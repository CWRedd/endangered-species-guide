Identification Tips

📸

Take Clear Photos

Multiple angles, good lighting, and close-ups of distinctive features help with accurate identification.

📍

Note the Location

Habitat information (forest, grassland, water) is crucial for narrowing down possibilities.

⏰

Record the Time

Season and time of day affect which species you're likely to encounter.

🔍

Look for Details

Distinctive markings, colors, size, and behavior are key to identification.

📚

Compare Similar Species

Some species look very alike. Check field guides for look-alikes to be sure.

👥

Ask the Community

Post on iNaturalist and let experienced observers help confirm your identification.
