Quick Identification Guide

Birds

  • Size: Compare to common reference (sparrow, robin, hawk)
  • Color patterns: Note distinctive markings and colors
  • Behavior: Flying, perching, feeding patterns
  • Habitat: Forest, open field, water, urban area
Bird Identification Help

Reptiles & Amphibians

  • Body type: Snake, lizard, turtle, frog, salamander
  • Scale patterns: Smooth, keeled, or other texture
  • Head shape: Distinctive features
  • Location found: Water, forest floor, tree, etc.
GA Reptile Guide

Insects & Spiders

  • Number of legs: Insects (6), spiders (8), other
  • Wing presence: Presence and type of wings
  • Color & patterns: Distinctive markings
  • Size: Approximate length
Insects on iNaturalist

Plants & Flora

  • Leaf shape: Simple, compound, palmate
  • Flower color & form: Distinctive features
  • Stem type: Vines, shrubs, trees
  • Habitat: Forest, field, wetland, urban
