Invasive species in Georgia

Species that have been introduced to Georgia and pose threats to native ecosystems and biodiversity.

Flora

Japanese Honeysuckle

Lonicera japonica

A fast-growing vine that covers native vegetation and prevents seedling growth. One of Georgia's most problematic invasive plants.

View on iNaturalist Learn more
Fauna

Feral Hog

Sus scrofa

Causes significant damage to forests and wetlands through rooting behavior. Competes with native wildlife for resources and destroys habitat.

View on iNaturalist Learn more
Aquatic

Lionfish

Pterois volitans / P. miles

Predatory fish found in coastal Georgia waters. No natural predators; preys on native fish and reduces biodiversity in marine ecosystems.

View on iNaturalist NOAA Resources
Flora

Kudzu

Pueraria montana

A rapidly spreading vine that smothers native plants and trees. Can grow up to one foot per day during growing season.

Native species of Georgia

Common species naturally found in Georgia ecosystems.

Fauna

Eastern Box Turtle

Terrapene carolina

A terrestrial turtle found throughout Georgia. Important for seed dispersal and population health is a good indicator of forest ecosystem quality.

View on iNaturalist Learn more
Fauna

Black Bear

Ursus americanus

Georgia's largest land mammal. Populations in the Appalachian region are recovering. An apex predator and important part of forest ecosystems.

View on iNaturalist Georgia Wildlife
Flora

Southern Magnolia

Magnolia grandiflora

A native evergreen tree with large fragrant flowers. Important for wildlife habitat and commonly found in Georgia's mixed forests.

View on iNaturalist Learn more
Fauna

Red-tailed Hawk

Buteo jamaicensis

A large raptor and common sight in Georgia. Uses open areas and forests for hunting. Excellent indicator of habitat health.
