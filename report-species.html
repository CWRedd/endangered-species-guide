Report a Species Sighting

Submit your observation to help track Georgia's wildlife and invasive species

How to Report

Have you spotted an organism in Georgia? Use the form below to gather your information, then submit your observation to the appropriate database to contribute to citizen science research and invasive species tracking.

Observation Details
Enter the common or scientific name if you know it, or describe what you saw
Location & Date
The more specific the better for tracking purposes
Details
Include details like size, color patterns, behavior, or anything else distinctive
If you have a photo uploaded elsewhere, paste the direct link here
Contact Information
We may contact you if we need more information about your sighting
Where to Submit

What Happens Next?

1. Review Your Data

Check that all information is accurate and complete. Photos and precise locations are especially helpful.

2. Submit to Database

You'll be directed to iNaturalist, EDDMapS, or the Georgia DNR to complete your official submission.

3. Community Review

Other naturalists and experts will help verify your identification and add insights to your observation.

4. Contribute to Science

Your data helps track species populations, monitor invasive species spread, and inform conservation efforts.
