What Happens Next?

1. Review Your Data

Check that all information is accurate and complete. Photos and precise locations are especially helpful for identification.

2. Submit to Database

You'll be directed to iNaturalist, EDDMapS, or the Georgia DNR to complete your official submission with your prepared data.

3. Community Review

Other naturalists and experts will help verify your identification and add insights to your observation.

4. Contribute to Science

Your data helps track species populations, monitor invasive species spread, and inform conservation efforts in Georgia.
